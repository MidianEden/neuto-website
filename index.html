FAQ

What do I actually do in Neuto?

You play short daily rounds that train memory, focus, language, math, and reasoning. The app keeps adjusting so it does not feel like the same drill every day.

How much time does it take?

Most people can get through a useful session in about 5 to 10 minutes a day.

Do I need to be good at brain games already?

No. Neuto starts from your baseline and gets harder as you improve.

Is Neuto anti-AI?

No. It is for people who use AI and do not want to feel mentally rusty without it.
